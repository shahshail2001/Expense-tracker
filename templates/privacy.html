Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 28, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when you create an account, including your email address, password, and any profile information. Additionally, we collect the expense data you enter into the app, such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Spendly service. We use your expense logs to generate spending patterns and monthly summaries for your personal review. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

We store your data on secure servers. While we implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not currently share your data with third-party services. If we integrate any third-party tools in the future, we will update this policy and notify you accordingly.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the data we collect, please contact us at support@spendly.example.com.
